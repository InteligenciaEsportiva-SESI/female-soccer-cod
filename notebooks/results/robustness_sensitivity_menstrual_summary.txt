Interpretation:
  β for continuous predictors: change in outcome per +1 SD of predictor.
  β for menstrual_binary: mean-difference between menstruating (= 1)
  and not-bleeding (= 0) athletes, holding mechanical predictors at mean.

Inferential framing:
  This arm is a ROBUSTNESS CHECK on the direction-stability of the main
  3-predictor mechanical paradox — not an inferential test of menstrual
  status. With n = 22 and 4 predictors (5.5 obs per predictor, below
  Harrell's 10:1 rule), the 4-predictor model is intentionally
  over-parameterised; the headline read is whether the three mechanical
  β estimates preserve sign and magnitude under adjustment (see the
  'Direction held?' column).

Prior calibration:
  β ~ Normal(0, 1) is weakly informative for SD-standardised continuous
  predictors. For the raw 0/1 binary covariate the prior is scale-dependent
  on the outcome's residual SD:
    Sprint (residual SD ≈ 0.09 s): prior wide; minimal shrinkage.
    CODD   (residual SD ≈ 4.7 %): prior SD = 1 < OLS SE ≈ 2.8; posterior
                                  median is moderately shrunk toward 0.
  Implication for CODD menstrual_binary: OLS β ≈ −1.9 vs Bayes median ≈ 0
  reflects prior-driven shrinkage, not contradiction. Both arms agree
  the binary covariate is not credibly distinct from zero (OLS p > 0.5,
  Bayes pd ≈ 0.5).
